What a data principal is sent when she gives or withdraws consent.

**4 emails.** The subject and body below are the defaults; the Privacy Office may replace the words of any of them in **Message templates**, and the variables are filled in when each is sent. Every one is sent by a Celery task through `deliver()` after the transaction that caused it has committed, from `SENDER_EMAIL`, laid out in the platform's email template.

<a id="consent-receipt"></a>
## 1. Consent receipt

Key `consent_receipt` · channels: email, sms

| | |
|---|---|
| When | Her copy of the record, sent after consent is given through a link. |
| To | The data principal who gave consent, at her primary contact. |
| CC | Never - it is written to one person about her own data; a copy would disclose it. |
| Attachment | Her consent record (consent-record.txt): what she agreed to and when, the notice version and its fingerprint. |
| Sent by | `cmp.notifications.send_consent_receipt` (`backend/api/src/cmp/tasks/notifications/consent.py:50`) |
| Queued from | `backend/api/src/cmp/domain/consent/service.py:627` |
| Variables | `project_name` (The project the consent is for.), `reference` (The consent record's reference.), `purposes` (The purposes agreed to, one per line, each starting with a dash.), `purpose_count` (How many purposes were agreed to.), `withdraw_url` (Where she can review or withdraw.), `organisation` (The organisation's name (ORGANISATION_NAME).) |

**Subject:** `Your consent record for {project_name}`

**Body:**

```text
Thank you. We have recorded your decision for {project_name}.

You agreed to:
{purposes}

Your record reference is {reference}. Keep this message: it is your copy of what you agreed to, and you can quote the reference if you ever ask us about it.

You can review or withdraw at any time, and withdrawing is as easy as agreeing was:
{withdraw_url}

Withdrawing stops future processing for the purposes you withdraw. It does not by itself delete data already collected; if that is what you want, ask for erasure from the same page.

If you were not expecting this message, please tell the Privacy Office at {organisation}.
```

**SMS:**

```text
{organisation}: your consent for {project_name} is recorded ({purpose_count} purpose(s)). Ref {reference}. Review or withdraw any time: {withdraw_url}
```

## 2. Re-consent request (HR activity)

Key `hr_reconsent_request` · channels: email, sms

| | |
|---|---|
| When | Sent to every participant with a current consent when an HR activity moves to a template version Legal flagged as a material change. Her consent stands until she decides. |
| To | Each participant with a current consent on the activity, at her primary contact. |
| CC | Never - it carries a code or a link, and a copy would hand it to somebody else. |
| Attachment | None. |
| Sent by | `cmp.notifications.send_reconsent_request` (`backend/api/src/cmp/tasks/notifications/consent.py:100`) |
| Queued from | `backend/api/src/cmp/domain/hr/activities.py:448` |
| Variables | `activity_name` (The activity's name.), `change_summary` (What changed: the template and its new version.), `link_url` (The activity's consent link, where she decides again.), `organisation` (The organisation's name (ORGANISATION_NAME).) |

**Subject:** `Your consent for {activity_name} needs renewing`

**Body:**

```text
The terms for {activity_name} have changed ({change_summary}), and the Legal office has asked everyone taking part to look at them again.

Your existing consent stands until you decide. To read the new notice and decide, open:
{link_url}

You can also withdraw at any time from your consents page.

If you were not expecting this message, please tell the Privacy Office at {organisation}.
```

**SMS:**

```text
{organisation}: the terms for {activity_name} have changed. Your consent stands until you decide; read and decide here: {link_url}
```

<a id="hr-withdrawal-notice"></a>
## 3. Withdrawal notice to an HR activity's respondents

Key `hr_withdrawal_notice` · channels: email

| | |
|---|---|
| When | Sent when a participant withdraws on an HR activity, to its HR PoC and the respondent of each processor holding the category's data, so they stop processing. Refers to the participant by employee ID or principal ID, never by name. |
| To | Each respondent of the activity: its HR PoC and the respondent of every live category processor (the HR PoC where none is named), once per account. |
| CC | None by default. The Privacy Office may add up to 5 addresses in **Message templates → Copy to** (a team mailbox, an approvals inbox). |
| Attachment | None. |
| Sent by | `cmp.notifications.send_hr_withdrawal_notice` (`backend/api/src/cmp/tasks/notifications/withdrawal.py:68`) |
| Queued from | `backend/api/src/cmp/domain/consent/service.py:678` |
| Variables | `activity_name` (The activity's name.), `category_name` (The activity's category.), `principal_ref` (The participant's employee ID, or her principal ID when none is on record.), `stopped` (The purposes withdrawn, comma-separated.), `continuing` (The purposes still in force, comma-separated, or 'none'.), `consent_reference` (The withdrawal record's reference.), `register_url` (The activity's register in the console.), `organisation` (The organisation's name (ORGANISATION_NAME).) |

**Subject:** `Consent withdrawn on {activity_name} - stop processing for {principal_ref}`

**Body:**

```text
A participant in {activity_name} ({category_name}) has withdrawn consent.

Participant: {principal_ref}
Withdrawn: {stopped}
Still in force: {continuing}
Record: {consent_reference}

Stop processing the data collected under the withdrawn purposes. Withdrawal does not by itself erase what was collected; an erasure, if she asks for one, comes as a separate request.

The activity's register:
{register_url}
```

## 4. Withdrawal confirmation

Key `withdrawal_confirmation` · channels: email, sms

| | |
|---|---|
| When | Sent when she withdraws some or all purposes. Says what stops and what continues. |
| To | The data principal who withdrew, at her primary contact. |
| CC | Never - it carries a code or a link, and a copy would hand it to somebody else. |
| Attachment | None. |
| Sent by | `cmp.notifications.send_withdrawal_confirmation` (`backend/api/src/cmp/tasks/notifications/withdrawal.py:37`) |

| Variables | `reference` (The new record's reference.), `stopped` (The purposes withdrawn, comma-separated.), `continuing` (The purposes still in force, comma-separated, or empty.), `continuing_note` (A sentence about what continues, or empty when nothing does.), `rights_url` (Where to ask for erasure.), `organisation` (The organisation's name (ORGANISATION_NAME).) |

**Subject:** `Your withdrawal has been recorded`

**Body:**

```text
We have recorded your withdrawal. Reference: {reference}.

Stopped: {stopped}.
{continuing_note}

Processing for the purposes you withdrew ceases within a reasonable period. The earlier record is kept as evidence of what was agreed at the time; it permits nothing further.

Data already collected is not deleted by a withdrawal. To ask for erasure, make a rights request:
{rights_url}

If you were not expecting this message, please tell the Privacy Office at {organisation}.
```

**SMS:**

```text
{organisation}: your withdrawal is recorded. Stopped: {stopped}. Ref {reference}. To ask for erasure of data already collected: {rights_url}
```
