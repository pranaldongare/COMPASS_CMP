**2 emails.** The subject and body below are the defaults; the Privacy Office may replace the words of any of them in **Message templates**, and the variables are filled in when each is sent. ## 1. Consent receipt

Key `consent_receipt` · channels: email, sms

| | |
|---|---|
| When | Her copy of the record, sent after consent is given through a link. |
| To | The data principal who gave consent, at her primary contact. |
| CC | Never - it is written to one person about her own data; a copy would disclose it. |
| Attachment | Her consent record (consent-record.txt): what she agreed to and when, the notice version and its fingerprint. |
| Sent by | `cmp.notifications.send_consent_receipt` (`backend/api/src/cmp/tasks/notifications/consent.py:50`) |
| Queued from | `backend/api/src/cmp/domain/consent/service.py:613` |
| Variables | `project_name` (The project the consent is for.), `reference` (The consent record's reference.), `purposes` (The purposes agreed to, one per line, each starting with a dash.), `purpose_count` (How many purposes were agreed to.), `withdraw_url` (Where she can review or withdraw.), `organisation` (The organisation's name (ORGANISATION_NAME).) |

**Subject:** `Your consent record for {project_name}`

**Body:**

```text
Thank you. We have recorded your decision for {project_name}.

You agreed to:
{purposes}

Your record reference is {reference}. Keep this message: it is your copy of what you agreed to, and you can quote the reference if you ever ask us about it.

You can review or withdraw at any time, and withdrawing is as easy as agreeing was:
{withdraw_url}

Withdrawing stops future processing for the purposes you withdraw. It does not by itself delete data already collected; if that is what you want, ask for erasure from the same page.

If you were not expecting this message, please tell the Privacy Office at {organisation}.
```

**SMS:**

```text
{organisation}: your consent for {project_name} is recorded ({purpose_count} purpose(s)). Ref {reference}. Review or withdraw any time: {withdraw_url}
```

## 2. Withdrawal confirmation

Key `withdrawal_confirmation` · channels: email, sms

| | |
|---|---|
| When | Sent when she withdraws some or all purposes. Says what stops and what continues. |
| To | The data principal who withdrew, at her primary contact. |
| CC | Never - it carries a code or a link, and a copy would hand it to somebody else. |
| Attachment | None. |
| Sent by | `cmp.notifications.send_withdrawal_confirmation` (`backend/api/src/cmp/tasks/notifications/withdrawal.py:37`) |
| Queued from | `backend/api/src/cmp/domain/consent/service.py:742` |
| Variables | `reference` (The new record's reference.), `stopped` (The purposes withdrawn, comma-separated.), `continuing` (The purposes still in force, comma-separated, or empty.), `continuing_note` (A sentence about what continues, or empty when nothing does.), `rights_url` (Where to ask for erasure.), `organisation` (The organisation's name (ORGANISATION_NAME).) |

**Subject:** `Your withdrawal has been recorded`

**Body:**

```text
We have recorded your withdrawal. Reference: {reference}.

Stopped: {stopped}.
{continuing_note}

Processing for the purposes you withdrew ceases within a reasonable period. The earlier record is kept as evidence of what was agreed at the time; it permits nothing further.

Data already collected is not deleted by a withdrawal. To ask for erasure, make a rights request:
{rights_url}

If you were not expecting this message, please tell the Privacy Office at {organisation}.
```

**SMS:**

```text
{organisation}: your withdrawal is recorded. Stopped: {stopped}. Ref {reference}. To ask for erasure of data already collected: {rights_url}
```
