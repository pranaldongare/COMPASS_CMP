**12 emails.** The subject and body below are the defaults; the Privacy Office may replace the words of any of them in **Message templates**, and the variables are filled in when each is sent. Every one is sent by a Celery task through `deliver()` after the transaction that caused it has committed, from `SENDER_EMAIL`, laid out in the platform's email template.
