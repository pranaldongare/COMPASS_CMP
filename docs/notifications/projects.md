# Projects and approvals: every email

[Notification strategy](README.md) · [Implementation plan](implementation-plan.md) · [Every message](../email/messages.md)

Generated by `docs/tools/generate-email-docs.py` from the code. Do not edit by hand.

A project's way from draft to approved: submitted, approved, sent back, closed, and the collectors named on it.

**5 emails.** The subject and body below are the defaults; the Privacy Office may replace the words of any of them in **Message templates**, and the variables are filled in when each is sent. Every one is sent by a Celery task through `deliver()` after the transaction that caused it has committed, from `SENDER_EMAIL`, laid out in the platform's email template.

## 1. A project is waiting for approval

| | |
|---|---|
| When | Sent to the Privacy Office when an R&D User submits a project for approval. The project, who submitted it, and where to review it. |
| To | Every active Data Protection Officer, at their primary email. |
| CC | None by default. The Privacy Office may add up to 5 addresses in **Message templates → Copy to** (a team mailbox, an approvals inbox). |
| Attachment | None. |
| Sent by | `cmp.notifications.send_project_submitted` (`backend/api/src/cmp/tasks/notifications/alerts.py:36`) |
| Queued from | - |
| Variables | `project_name` (The project.), `submitted_by` (The R&D User who submitted it.), `project_url` (The project in the console.), `organisation` (The organisation's name (ORGANISATION_NAME).) |

**Subject:** `{project_name} is waiting for your approval`

**Body:**

